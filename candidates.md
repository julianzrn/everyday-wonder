# Candidates — Everyday Wonder

Competition: `u-pangg7-everyday-wonder-two-days-to-find-the-line-970432d3`
Brief: one line of everyday wonder — a small, ordinary scene seen closely enough to become strange and lovely.

## Candidate 1 — SELECTED (150 characters)

Every night the streetlight outside my window practices being the moon for an audience of one moth, and the moth, every night, believes it completely.

Why it wins: it has a complete tiny drama — an understudy (the streetlight) rehearsing a role it will never officially play, and a single audience member whose belief makes the performance real. 'Practices' and 'believes it completely' mirror each other across the line, and the repetition of 'every night' makes it a ritual rather than a one-off image.
